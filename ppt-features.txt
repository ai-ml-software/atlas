# ALTUS HK&P — PHP LMS UPGRADE
## Complete Feature List & Detailed Brief

### 1. Executive Summary
The existing PHP LMS must be upgraded into a **white-label, bilingual, multi-tenant, AI-enabled hospitality knowledge and performance platform** called **altus Hospitality Knowledge & Performance**.  
It must serve independent hotels, serviced apartments, and SME establishments across Saudi Arabia and the Arab region, without the cost and complexity of global-chain systems.  
It must convert three decades of hotel expertise and operating standards into a structured digital system for **learning, knowledge management, and performance measurement**.  
The platform must deliver the promise:  
> “The right knowledge, to the right person, at the right time, with clear evidence of learning and improvement.”

It must fuse consulting IP with software, so knowledge stays institutionalised, versioned, continuously updated, and becomes a digital asset of the institution — not of departing staff.

### 4. Detailed Technical Upgrade Brief for PHP LMS

#### 4.1 Recommended Stack
- **PHP 8.2/8.3** or latest stable.
- **Laravel 11** or Symfony 7 (Laravel preferred for rapid multi-tenant SaaS).
- **Database**: MySQL 8 or PostgreSQL 16 (PostgreSQL preferred for JSONB, full-text search, pgvector for AI).
- **Caching**: Redis.
- **Queue**: Laravel Horizon / Redis.
- **Search**: Meilisearch, Elasticsearch, or PostgreSQL full-text.
- **AI/RAG**: Vector database (pgvector, Pinecone, Weaviate) + OpenAI/Anthropic/local LLM.
- **Frontend**: Blade + Livewire + Alpine.js, or Vue/React with Inertia.
- **Mobile**: PWA first, then React Native/Flutter if needed.
- **DevOps**: Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD (GitHub Actions/GitLab CI).
- **Monitoring**: Sentry, Laravel Telescope, Prometheus/Grafana.

#### 4.2 Architecture
- **Multi-tenant**: Single database with `tenant_id` or separate database per property.  
  - Recommended: single DB, tenant scoped, with row-level security.
- **White-label**:  
  - Custom domain/subdomain mapping.  
  - Theme engine: logo, colors, fonts, email templates, certificates.  
  - Per-property settings table.
- **API-first**:  
  - RESTful API for all modules.  
  - Webhooks for PMS/POS/channel integrations.
- **Modular monolith**:  
  - Modules: Auth, Tenants, Properties, Users, Roles, Courses, Tracks, Modules, Lessons, Assessments, Certifications, AI Assistant, Search, Analytics, Reports, Notifications, Branding, Admin, CMS, Versioning, Security, Integrations.

#### 4.3 Database Schema (Key Tables)
- `tenants` (properties)
- `users` (with `tenant_id`, role)
- `roles`, `permissions`, `role_user`
- `domains` (10 professional domains)
- `tracks`
- `modules`
- `lessons`
- `assessments`
- `questions`
- `answers`
- `certifications`
- `user_progress`
- `competency_reports`
- `ai_documents` (approved content)
- `ai_conversations`
- `kpi_metrics`
- `dashboards`
- `integrations`
- `audit_logs`
- `versions` (content versioning)

#### 4.4 Learning Engine

---

# 1. PRODUCT OBJECTIVE

Upgrade the existing PHP LMS into a production-ready, bilingual, multi-property, white-label **Hospitality Knowledge & Performance Platform** for Altus Advisory.

The system must evolve from a conventional LMS that primarily tracks course completion into a complete system that connects:

**Knowledge → Learning → Assessment → Competency → Gap Identification → Corrective Action → Reassessment → Readiness → Certification → Operational Performance**

The core product promise is:

> **“The right knowledge, to the right person, at the right time, with clear evidence of learning and improvement.”**

The platform must institutionalise Altus Advisory's hospitality operating knowledge so that knowledge remains inside the client organisation rather than disappearing when employees leave.

The system must support:

* Independent hotels
* Serviced apartments
* Hospitality SMEs
* Hotel groups
* Multi-property portfolios
* Client organisations
* Altus internal teams
* Property management teams
* Individual learners
* Supervisors and assessors
* Executives, owners and decision-makers

The system must be designed around:

> **From Knowledge to Performance**

---

# 2. CORE PRODUCT DIFFERENTIATOR

The system must not be treated as a generic LMS.

A conventional LMS generally measures:

* Course assigned
* Course opened
* Lesson completed
* Test completed
* Certificate generated

Altus requires a deeper model.

The system must measure:

* What the employee was expected to know
* What knowledge they actually consumed
* What they understood
* What they can demonstrate
* What competencies they possess
* Where competency gaps exist
* What corrective actions were assigned
* Whether improvement occurred
* Whether reassessment was successful
* Whether the employee is operationally ready
* Whether the property/team is achieving required standards

Therefore, **completion must never automatically equal competence**.

# 28. GOVERNED AI ASSISTANT

This is a major differentiator.

Implement an AI assistant that answers questions using only approved Altus/client knowledge.

The AI must use retrieval from the approved knowledge base.

AI must NOT freely invent policy.

Required behaviour:

1. User asks a question.
2. System determines user's tenant/property/role.
3. System retrieves authorised approved content.
4. AI generates answer using retrieved content.
5. Answer should identify the source content where possible.
6. If approved knowledge does not contain the answer, AI must clearly state that the approved knowledge base does not provide the required answer rather than inventing a policy.

The AI must not use:

* Draft documents
* Deleted documents
* Rejected documents
* Unapproved documents
* Other client properties' private documents

unless explicitly authorised.

# 87. MOST IMPORTANT PRODUCT RULES

The development team must treat these as non-negotiable:

**1. Training completion is not competency.**

**2. Competency must have evidence.**

**3. Gaps must lead to actions.**

**4. Actions must be reassessable.**

**5. Readiness must be evidence-based.**

**6. Institutional knowledge must survive employee turnover.**

**7. AI must answer only from governed, approved knowledge.**

**8. One property must never see another property's restricted data.**

**9. Arabic and English must be first-class experiences.**

**10. Every client property must support its own brand.**

**11. Historical records must remain auditable.**

**12. Existing production data must be preserved through controlled migration.**

**13. Executive performance information must be separated from learner information.**

**14. Corporate/advisory claims must remain editable content, not hardcoded application logic.**

**15. Every major feature must work end-to-end before being considered complete.**
Below is the continuation, moving from the business/product specification into the **actual implementation blueprint**: modules, screens, database entities, workflows, APIs, security, AI architecture, and deployment requirements.
