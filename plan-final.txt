# Hospitality Academy — Laravel 100% Completion Master Plan

Based on your requested scope, the system should be built as a **complete, production-ready Hospitality LMS + SOP Knowledge Hub + Hospitality Academy + SEO/CMS + Competitor Intelligence platform**, not as a simple course website.

The implementation standard should be:

> **No TODO, no PARTIAL, no FAILED, no placeholder page, no fake button, no dead sidebar item, and no “coming soon” functionality. Every documented requirement must end in a working implementation with automated tests and browser verification.**

The available Dyafa material already establishes a strong Saudi-market direction: bilingual EN/AR, full RTL, structured SEO, mobile performance, measurable CTAs, and hospitality-specific content rather than generic business content.  The existing Saudi hospitality keyword research also identifies high-intent areas such as hotel management software, compliance, training/operational content, and Saudi hospitality search intent.

## Product name

**Hospitality Academy**

| Module              | Purpose                                                                |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |

**Analytics + competency matrix + training compliance + reporting.**
